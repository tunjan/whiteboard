Spatial Grouping

Strict 4px grid. 2px (gap-0.5) intra-group, 10px (gap-2.5) inter-group. No internal divider lines; spacing alone establishes hierarchy.

Calm State Feedback

Single accent color (#27604A moss) for speaking/active state. High-contrast ink for muted. Zero loud warning sirens; soft wash for error recovery.

A11y & Controls

All controls ≥ 40px (size-10). Icons standardized at 20px (size-5) stroke 1.7. Explicit aria-pressed, aria-expanded, and polite live regions.
