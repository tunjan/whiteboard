Alternative 1: Architectural Rationale

Calm Minimalist
Zero Separator Lines

Replaces mechanical 1px dividers with calibrated whitespace rhythm. Peer items sit at gap-0.5 (2px); semantic groups breathe at gap-2.5 (10px).

No Overline Microcopy

Eliminated the uppercase MORE TOOLS label. The dropdown is opened intentionally by clicking the ellipsis button; repeating the obvious adds visual chatter.

Target Size Compliance

Every row is strictly h-10 (40px) with px-2.5. Completely satisfies WCAG 2.5.5 / 2.5.8 touch target ergonomics while staying slim at 240px width.

Unified 20px Stroke 1.7 Glyphs

All iconography conforms to the Calm UI stroke geometry (stroke: 1.7, round caps, round joins). Eliminates mixed visual weights across shapes and tools.
